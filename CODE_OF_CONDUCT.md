# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by mistakes, and learn.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report issues privately to the maintainers via the contact on the GitHub profile.
All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
