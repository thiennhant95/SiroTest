# Personas

## Manual Tester
Needs record, visual editing, assertions, variables, run and readable failures. Must not need TypeScript.

## Automation QA
Needs advanced locators, reusable actions, data-driven cases, suites, tags, artifacts and deterministic runs.

## Developer
Needs generated Playwright, custom code/actions, Trace Viewer, raw errors, environment control and normal Playwright interoperability.

## Admin
Manages projects, members, environments/secrets and operational settings.

## Viewer
Read-only: watches runs, artifacts, audit and reports. Cannot write, run, approve or delete anything (403 on writes).
